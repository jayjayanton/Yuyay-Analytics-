Air Quality & Public Health — Central America

NASA DEVELOP project team; Jay Oliver, data processing and regression analysis contributor

Compared satellite-derived aerosol optical depth with ground PM2.5 observations, including analyses for El Salvador, Guatemala and Panama. The project documents results and limitations in team poster and presentation materials.
